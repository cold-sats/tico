# About Acme

Acme is a small company that sells project-tracking software to studios: design, video and development
shops that run a handful of client projects at a time. The product is called Acme Projects.

## Who does what

- **Ana Rivera** is the founder and owner. She approves anything over the limits in the finance and pricing docs.
- **Ben Okafor** is head of support and owns the support queue and the help centre.
- **Cara Mendes** is the marketing lead and owns the website and the launch calendar.
- **Dana Reyes** is head of engineering and owns releases and the on-call rotation.

## Where the team works

Everyone works remotely across US time zones. The company calendar and all times in these docs are Pacific time.
