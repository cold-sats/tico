# Pricing

Acme Projects has three plans, all per workspace and billed monthly unless the customer chooses annual billing.

| Plan | Price per month | Projects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Studio | $29 | up to 5 |
| Team | $79 | up to 25 |
| Agency | $199 | unlimited |

## Annual billing

Paying annually gives two months free: twelve months for the price of ten.

## Discounts

A salesperson may give up to 10% off without approval. Nonprofits get 20% off, and that also needs no
approval. Any other discount, or anything over 10%, goes to Ana Rivera before it is offered.
