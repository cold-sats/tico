# Support hours and response times

Support is open Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm Pacific. It is closed on weekends and on US public holidays.

## First response times

- **Team and Agency plans**: a first reply within 4 business hours.
- **Studio plan**: a first reply within 1 business day.

## Outages

An outage is announced on the status page first. The on-call engineer is paged automatically; the head of
engineering is the escalation. Support does not promise a fix time during an outage.
