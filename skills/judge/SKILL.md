# Judge is now Decisions

This skill was renamed. Read [`skills/decisions/SKILL.md`](../decisions/SKILL.md): a decision
question (yes/no, choice or score, the format of OpenRouter's Decisions API) answered with
probabilities you act on with thresholds. The old `hub_judge` tool and `hub judge` command are
kept as aliases of `hub_decisions` and `hub decisions`.
