# Review one reply

Triggered by a task naming one ticket or reply, and used for each reply in
`playbooks/weekly-reply-review.md`. Budget 5 minutes. The outcome is one scored entry.

---

## 1. Read the whole thread

Read the customer's message, every earlier reply and the one being reviewed. Read the customer's
question first and decide what a complete answer would contain before reading the reply.

## 2. Check accuracy against a written source

Find the standing answer or policy in the linked docs or `knowledge/`. A claim you cannot verify is
marked "unverified", never "wrong" and never "right".

## 3. Score

Each criterion 1, 2 or 3 as `knowledge/scorecard.md` defines it:
- **Accuracy**: the facts and steps are right for this customer's plan and situation.
- **Tone**: warm and plain, acknowledges the problem, does not blame the customer.
- **Completeness**: answers everything asked, in one reply where it can.
- **Policy**: no promise, discount or disclosure outside what is allowed.
- **Next step**: the customer knows what happens next and who does it.
Write one reason and one quoted sentence for any score below 2 or of 3.

## 4. Record

Add the entry to `knowledge/reviews/YYYY-MM-DD.md` with the ticket reference and date, no customer
personal details. If the reply promised or disclosed something it should not have, stop and follow
"Escalating" in AGENT.md at once.
